Terms

How it works

  1. Request DM me on X (Twitter) or Boosty with your references and idea.
  2. Booking I confirm the price and deadline. After 100% payment your piece goes into the queue.
  3. Sketch & revisions I send the sketch for approval. Two free revisions at this stage.
  4. Delivery Final PNG or JPG at 300 dpi. The .clip source file on request.

I draw

  • Original characters
  • Fan art
  • Portraits and couples
  • Fantasy: elves, armour, costumes
  • Everyday, moody scenes
  • NSFW — discussed in DMs

I don’t draw

  • Complex mecha and vehicles
  • Hateful or offensive content
  • Art for NFTs or AI training
  • Copies of other artists’ styles
How long does it take?

Usually 1–3 weeks depending on the size and the queue. I’ll give you an exact deadline before payment.

What about revisions?

Two free revisions at the sketch stage. Small fixes after colouring are fine; big changes at that point are paid extra.

Can I get a refund?

Full refund before I start the sketch, 50% after the sketch is done. No refunds once colouring has started.

How can I use the art?

Personal use: avatars, banners, posting with credit, printing for yourself. Selling, merch or ads count as commercial use (×2).

Can I post the art?

Yes, please credit me. I may also post the piece in my portfolio — tell me if you want it kept private.

How do I pay?

100% upfront after we agree on the details. I’ll send payment options in DMs.
